Questions

Will the free tier ever get worse?

No. Everything on the free tier today stays free. We'll never move existing free features behind a paywall. If you can do it today for free, you'll always be able to.

What happens when premium launches?

Existing active users will receive a 30-day free trial of premium automatically — no credit card required. You'll have time to try every feature before deciding.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from Settings and you'll retain premium access until the end of your billing period. No questions, no friction.

What's the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual is $29.99 — equivalent to $2.50/month, saving you 37% versus monthly. And if you're a Founding Member, your rate is locked at $19.99/year regardless of future pricing.

Why is Spin Vinyl free at all?

Because the vision isn't a business — it's a community. Passionate listeners and collectors sharing their love of music with each other. Not for money, not for fame. Just for the love of it. Premium exists to keep the lights on and build more of what you love.
