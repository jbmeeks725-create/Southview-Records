          Import your collection

          How to export your collection from Discogs
          1. Go to discogs.com and sign in
          2. Click your username in the top right → Collection
          3. Scroll to the very bottom of your collection page
          4. Click "Export" — this generates a CSV file and emails it to you, or downloads directly depending on your browser
          5. Once downloaded, upload that CSV file below

          Your Discogs export includes artist, title, label, year, format, and media condition. SPIN VINYL maps all of these automatically.
